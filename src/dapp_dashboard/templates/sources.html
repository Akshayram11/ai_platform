What happens to your credentials

  • You type them here over HTTPS. This dashboard keeps them only while it creates the Secret: never in its storage, logs or pages.
  • They live in a DataOS Secret (dapp-src-NAME) owned by you. Pipelines get them from DataOS when they run; manifests only name the Secret.
  • Nobody else can use the source until you approve their request. Approving also lets them read the Secret, so connect with a separate read-only user on the source, not your own account.
  • Changed the password at the source? Use Replace credentials. Removing a source deletes its Secret too (unless you keep it).
